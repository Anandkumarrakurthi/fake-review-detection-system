Feature Descriptions

Rating

Product rating given by the reviewer (1-5 stars)

Sentiment Score

Sentiment polarity ranging from -1 (negative) to +1 (positive)

Helpful Vote Count

Number of users who found the review helpful

Verified Purchase

Whether the reviewer actually purchased the product (1=Yes, 0=No)

Review Length

Total character count of the review text

Word Count

Total number of words in the review

Fake Review Detection Patterns

Sentiment-Rating Mismatch

High positive sentiment with low rating or vice versa indicates potential fake review

Unverified Extremes

Unverified purchases with extreme ratings (1 or 5 stars) are suspicious

Length Anomalies

Unusually short or excessively long reviews may indicate spam

Zero Engagement

Lengthy reviews with zero helpful votes suggest low credibility

Moderate-Extreme Gap

Moderate sentiment with extreme ratings shows inconsistency

Pattern Combination

Multiple suspicious patterns combined trigger fake classification
